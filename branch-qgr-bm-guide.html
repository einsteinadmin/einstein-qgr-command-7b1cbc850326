How to Run Your Branch QGR

One page, one hour, your whole team. Everything you present lives in the Mover View of your branch scorecard.

Before the meeting (15 minutes of prep)

  1. Print Vision Summaries — one per mover. Vision Summary (Google Slides). Values and purpose stay blank on purpose — your team hand-writes those in the room.
  2. Open your scorecard from the table below and click the Mover toggle at the top. That's your whole presentation — no slides, no second tab.
  3. Read it once yourself. Know your branch's grade story, your Games result, and your eNPS trend before your team asks.

The meeting (~60 minutes)

  1. Best things — quick round: everyone shares one best thing from the quarter, work or life. (5–10 min)
  2. Scroll the Mover View top to bottom. It's built in presentation order: how the company did → quarter highlights → the money story (open the "sixth grade" explainer if the room wants it — it's good) → the numbers that matter to movers → your branch at a glance → eNPS + our response → Fleet Safety → Claims & Magic Moments → what customers said → Einstein Games results + prizes → Q3 theme. Read the room; linger where your crew has questions. (30–35 min)
  3. Vision Summary walk — hand out the printed sheets. Team hand-writes values + purpose from memory, then reveal and fill the gaps together. Walk the rest of the sheet section by section. (10 min)
  4. Mover accountability — comp review section on the scorecard: promotion path, what gets a mover promoted, what the Q3 theme asks of each person. (5–10 min)
  5. Close on purpose — read it aloud together off the Vision Summary. Questions welcome anytime: your BM, your regional, or regionalteam@einsteinmoving.com.
You present, they follow along. Screen shows the scorecard; hands hold the Vision Summary. Don't read every word — tell it like you'd tell a crew in the truck.
Stay in Mover View. The Manager view has leadership-level detail that isn't part of this meeting.
